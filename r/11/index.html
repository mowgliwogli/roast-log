  1. Drying

    0:00 to 4:05, 57% of the roast

    The beans go in green and damp. They dry out and turn yellow, and the roaster smells of hay and toast.

  2. Browning

    4:05 to 5:08, 15% of the roast

    Sugars start to brown, the way a crust forms on bread. Most of the coffee's aroma is built here.

  3. Development

    5:08 to 7:06, 28% of the roast

    Starts at first crack, when the beans pop like popcorn. The longer this lasts, the darker and more roasty the cup.
