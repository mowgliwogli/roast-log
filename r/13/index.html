  2. Browning

    Sugars start to brown, the way a crust forms on bread. Most of the coffee's aroma is built here.

    3:33 to 5:45, 38% of the roast

    Starts at first crack, when the beans pop like popcorn. The longer this lasts, the darker and more roasty the cup.
