Printing on a Brother QL

Choose the size that matches the roll in the printer, then choose Print label and pick the same paper size in the print window, with margins set to none. If the browser will not print it cleanly, choose Save as picture and drop the picture into Brother's P-touch Editor. It is drawn at 300 dots per inch, the printer's own resolution.

Printing on Avery 8160 sheets

Choose the Avery 8160 size, pick an unused position, then choose Make sheet PDF. The PDF opens in a new tab; print it on the label sheet with the scale at 100% (Actual size), not Fit to page. The first time, choose Test page for plain paper and print it on ordinary paper: held against the label sheet in front of a light, the grey boxes should sit on the labels.
